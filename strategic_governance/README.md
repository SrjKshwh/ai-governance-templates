# Strategic AI Governance Templates

This directory contains comprehensive frameworks and templates for strategic AI governance, including executive leadership structures, board oversight, ethics committees, and stakeholder engagement plans. These templates provide the strategic foundation for enterprise-wide AI governance and decision-making.
