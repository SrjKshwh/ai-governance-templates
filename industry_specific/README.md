## 🎯 Key Features

### Sector-Specific Regulatory Compliance
- **Cybersecurity:** NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, CISA advisories, NIS2 Directive
- **Financial Services:** Federal Reserve SR 11-7, OCC guidelines, EU AI Act, MiFID II
- **Healthcare:** FDA SaMD framework, EU MDR, clinical validation requirements
- **Manufacturing:** IEC 61508 functional safety, ISO 13849, ISO 26262 automotive safety

### Industry-Specific Risk Management
- **Tailored Risk Classifications:** Risk levels appropriate to each industry's safety and compliance requirements
- **Sector-Specific Controls:** Industry-appropriate mitigation strategies and control frameworks
- **Regulatory Integration:** Direct mapping to industry-specific regulations and standards

### Operational Excellence Integration
- **Industry Best Practices:** Sector-specific AI implementation patterns and success factors
- **Quality Standards:** Integration with industry quality management systems
- **Safety Frameworks:** Industry-appropriate safety architectures and validation procedures

## 🚀 Usage Guidelines

### Industry Selection Framework
1. **Regulatory Complexity:** Choose framework based on your industry's regulatory requirements
2. **Risk Profile:** Select based on safety criticality and compliance obligations
3. **AI Use Cases:** Match framework to your specific AI applications and deployment scenarios
4. **Organizational Maturity:** Consider your organization's AI governance maturity level

### Implementation Approach
- **Gap Analysis:** Use industry framework to identify current vs. required governance capabilities
- **Prioritization:** Focus on high-risk AI systems first based on industry risk classification
- **Integration:** Combine industry-specific requirements with general AI governance frameworks
- **Customization:** Adapt templates to your organization's specific regulatory and operational context

### Cross-Industry Applications
- **Multi-Industry Organizations:** Use multiple frameworks for different business units
- **Supply Chain Integration:** Apply appropriate frameworks to vendor and partner AI systems
- **Regulatory Harmonization:** Identify common requirements across different industry frameworks
