# AI Compliance & Legal Templates

This directory contains comprehensive legal and compliance templates for AI governance, covering regulatory frameworks, contract management, vendor due diligence, and certification requirements. These templates ensure organizations meet legal obligations while implementing responsible AI systems.

## 📋 Directory Contents

### Regulatory Compliance Frameworks
- **[`eu_ai_act_compliance_checklist.md`](eu_ai_act_compliance_checklist.md)** - EU AI Act compliance assessment checklist for high-risk AI systems
- **[`gdpr_ai_integration_guide.md`](gdpr_ai_integration_guide.md)** - Comprehensive GDPR integration guide for AI systems, including lawful basis, individual rights, and data protection impact assessments
- **[`nist_rmf_mapping_templates.md`](nist_rmf_mapping_templates.md)** - NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 implementation templates with governance, mapping, measurement, and management functions

### Standards & Certification
- **[`iso_42001_implementation_guide.md`](iso_42001_implementation_guide.md)** - ISO 42001 AI Management System implementation roadmap with phased approach

### Contract & Vendor Management
- **[`contract_risk_assessment.md`](contract_risk_assessment.md)** - Contract risk assessment framework for AI systems, covering liability, IP rights, SLAs, and termination provisions
- **[`vendor_due_diligence_templates.md`](vendor_due_diligence_templates.md)** - Comprehensive vendor due diligence framework for AI service providers, including technical, regulatory, and ethical assessments
